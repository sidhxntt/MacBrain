# Phases 6–9: evidence, graph, chat, memory, and live context

## Problem

Useful answers require more than finding text: results must rank exact and conceptual matches, preserve provenance, remain bounded for local inference, and distinguish a remembered preference from source-backed evidence.

## Treatment

Hybrid retrieval fuses lexical/vector candidates and may add bounded graph expansion. Evidence acceptance and citation validation preserve a source-to-card path. Streaming chat keeps session state local; memory remains an explicit, separately managed record. Live-Mac context is routed through narrowly scoped providers and safeguards rather than a background surveillance feed.

## Verification and boundary

Retrieval, evidence policy, graph, streaming, citations, prompts, memory, context, and real-backend suites cover focused contracts. No automated suite can certify factual correctness of every model sentence or the relevance of every user’s corpus; original-source inspection remains central to the product.
